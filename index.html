FAQ

Common questions

Can guests book rooms directly on WhatsApp?

Yes. Guests see room types and rates, choose dates and confirm with an advance payment link. You confirm availability from the inbox.

Do we have to leave OTAs like MakeMyTrip or Booking.com?

No. WhatsApp becomes an extra, commission-free channel for direct and repeat guests alongside your OTA listings.

Can it reply to enquiries at night?

Yes. The bot answers rates, availability and directions 24x7 and hands over to your staff when needed.

Can my reception staff reply from their phones?

Yes. Several team members can use the same WhatsApp inbox from phone or computer.

Do we need a new WhatsApp number?

A number connected to the WhatsApp Business API cannot be used in the regular WhatsApp app at the same time, so we recommend a new or virtual number. Our team helps with the full setup.

Will our number get banned for bulk messages?

Stay by Wamojo uses the official WhatsApp Business API from Meta, not unofficial bulk software. Message only guests who have opted in and follow Meta's messaging rules.

What are WhatsApp message charges?

Meta charges per delivered message: ₹0.18 for utility messages such as booking confirmations and check-in details, and ₹0.8631 for marketing messages such as weekend offers, plus 18% GST. These are separate from your plan.

How do I pay?

Pay securely online through Razorpay using UPI, debit or credit card, net banking or wallets. You receive a GST invoice for every payment.
